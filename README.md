# My Favorite Movie: Marvel Universe

**Iron Man** is the movie that kicked off the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). It follows Tony Stark, a billionaire inventor who becomes a superhero after building a suit of armor. The MCU now includes many characters and films, such as:

- *Avengers: Endgame*
- *Black Panther*
- *Spider-Man: Homecoming*

## Why I Love It:
- Great storytelling
- Iconic characters
- Action-packed sequences

Marvel has truly revolutionized the superhero genre!
